MediaWiki uses templates to both add standard banners and features to a page including notices about stub pages. What I'd like to see is the ability to tag a page as a "stub" or "incomplete" page and it then gets given a banner or other template item to let readers know it is incomplete and suggest editing the page to provide missing details.
