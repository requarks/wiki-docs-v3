There are currently __4 different headers__ at the top of content pages per default and none of them get hidden while scrolling down.
This means the space for actual content is quite a bit less compared to v2. In v2 only the first header stays in place.

I know you can get rid of the 4th header by disabling the "Comments" and "Backlinks" features, but I have not found a way to hide the extra headers 2 and 3 (see [Image](https://ibb.co/jvmX9Dcp)).

I would really like there to be an option to have these hide on scrolling or something similar.
