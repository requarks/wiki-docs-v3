I think it would be a nice addition to have the "Theme Toggle" button - currently only available for logged-in Users when they click on their Profile in the right corner - be available for logged out users / guests.

I believe a good position would be in the right corner next to the "Login" button, for Guests. The same Theme switcher toggle from the profile sub-menu of logged-in Users could be re-used and placed there for Guests.

Our wiki uses dark-mode as the default as it also fits the "brand", but some people need light-mode for accessibility reasons, and allowing that for Guests without forcing a log-in/profile would be great!
