It would be great to associate rules automatically on pages in certain paths. Say we have a folder with multiple routines that certain users create. We want these to have rules that send out notices to the document owners for review at fixed dates, based on when it was last updated. 

Having a default baseline on what rules should be present on a path would greatly lower administration
