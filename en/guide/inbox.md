# Overview

The **Inbox & Notifications** page is your one stop place for items requiring your attention. It can be accessed by clicking the :mdi:inbox-full: icon in the top-right corner of the site.

When a new message arrives, a badge count will automatically appear on the :mdi:inbox-full: icon in the site header.

# Inbox

The **Inbox** tab shows all notifications you received *(e.g. watched pages, comments, mentions, submissions pending your review, etc.)*.

Click on a message to go to the actionable item. It will automatically be marked as read.

You can filter by unread messages only from the top-right corner by activating the **Unread only** toggle. You can mark all messages as read by clicking the <kbd>:mdi:email-open-multiple-outline:</kbd> icon.

> [!TIP]
> You can customize which notifications you receive from your **Profile** :la:arrow-right: **Notifications** page.

# Watching

The **Watching** tab lists all the pages you are currently watching.

- To watch a page, click the :la:bell: icon in the page header.
- To stop watching a page, either:
    - On the page itself, click the :mdi:bell: icon in the page header.
    - From the **Watching** tab, click the :mdi:bell-off-outline: icon next to the desired page.

# Pending Review

The **Pending Review** tab lists all submissions from read-only users pending your review.

Refer to the [Suggest Edits](/guide/suggest-edits) page to learn how to setup approval rules to start accepting submissions and select who should review them.
