Content blocks are dynamic content sections you can include into your pages.

They are self-contained [Lit Web Components](https://lit.dev/).
While Wiki.js includes many popular blocks out of the box, it's quite easy to [create your own](/dev/blocks).

# Usage

- Look for the :mdi:toy-brick-plus: **Insert Block** button in most editors.
- For the Markdown syntax, check out the [Content Blocks](https://docs.js.wiki/guide/markdown#content-blocks) section of the Markdown reference guide.
